Healthcare Claims Intelligence & Risk Analytics Platform

Project 02 · Risk adjustmentComplete

Risk Adjustment Analytics

A simulated payer workflow for prioritizing members for documentation review when review capacity is limited.

5,000synthetic members
0.792ROC-AUC
0.526average precision
3.49×top-decile lift
34.9%top-decile capture

Business problem

A review team cannot examine every member, so it needs a transparent way to focus limited review capacity.

What I analyzed

RAF, HCC-style documentation, chronic burden, utilization, care gaps, member months, plan, and provider-group signals.

Key result

The top 10% of ranked members captured 34.9% of synthetic suspect cases—3.49 times the concentration expected from random review.

Business value

Turns model output into member worklists and provider action tables while keeping coding policy, clinical context, and human review in the process.

Tools

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Streamlit, and Plotly.
