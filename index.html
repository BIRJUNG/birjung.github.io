Featured project 01 · Claims analytics

Healthcare Claims Intelligence & Risk Analytics

An end-to-end healthcare analytics portfolio project that connects claims, membership, provider, quality, and risk signals in one governed analytical workflow.

Flagship
Business Problem

Claims teams need consistent answers across cost, utilization, readmissions, provider performance, and payment integrity—but one-off extracts can produce competing definitions.

Analysis

Built a conformed analytical layer, PMPM and member-month measures, provider peer groups, risk segments, validation checks, and targeted scoring workflows.

Key Results

On synthetic validation data, the high-cost model achieved 0.962 ROC-AUC, 0.759 PR-AUC, and 72.69% top-decile capture. Two of 180 providers were surfaced as anomaly-review candidates.

Business Decision

Supports prioritization for member review, readmission follow-up, provider investigation, and data-quality remediation. Scores are review signals—not automatic actions.

Featured project 02 · Risk adjustment

Healthcare Risk Adjustment Analytics

A simulated portfolio project that asks a practical question: when documentation-review capacity is limited, which members should a team review first?

Interactive
5,000synthetic members
0.792ROC-AUC
0.526Average Precision
3.49×Top-Decile Lift
34.9%Top-Decile Capture
Business Problem

A review team cannot examine every member. It needs a transparent queue that concentrates likely documentation opportunities.

Analysis

Combined RAF, HCC-style documentation, chronic burden, utilization, care gaps, and provider-group signals into ranked tiers.

Key Results

Reviewing the top 10% captured 34.9% of synthetic suspect cases—3.49 times the concentration expected from random selection.

Business Decision

Use the ranking to set a review starting point, then apply coding policy, clinical context, and human judgment before action.

Tools

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Streamlit, and Plotly.
